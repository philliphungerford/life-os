# life-os

## Introduction 

>This is your life operating system, branches are years and how they grow.

Life starts of with `YOU`. 

`YOU` experience life across `TIME`.

`TIME` is finite.

YOU `BEHAVE` which consumes `TIME`. 

Therefore, `YOUr` life is made up by your `BEHAVIOURS` across `TIME`. 

`BEHAVIOURS` are bundled into `DOMAINS`, for example, you go to school and behave and do things at school. These are separate to when you go home or go to piano lessons or when you are doing your hobbies like going to the gym or building/creating/playing. 

## LEVEL 1 - TIME MANAGEMENT

As `TIME` is a fundamental element, your first required tool is something that helps manage it - the `CALENDAR`.

When at work you will most likely receive a work `EMAIL` and `CALENDAR`. For personal not work related behaviours you will have a personal `CALENDAR`. 

Ideally you should be able to overlap calendars, on iOS you can see both your work and personal calendar overlaid so now you know how or what you will be doing across your personal and work life.

Knowing what you are doing at any given time gives you a sense of control and will ease your brain and ideally ease overwhelm. You will know what to do and when. 

Tip - have buffers in time management to give you breathing room not to rush. If it takes 30min to drive to work, block 1hour in your calendar, if you get there early, you will not feel stressed or start your day all anxious.
